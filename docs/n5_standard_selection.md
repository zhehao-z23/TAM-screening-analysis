# N5 Standard Selection

Candidate N5 libraries are compared with:

- Pearson correlation across all 5N PAM abundances
- Jensen-Shannon distance
- Shannon entropy
- Gini coefficient
- top1 and top10 abundance fractions
- pairwise scatter and MA plots

A good standard N5 library should have high average Pearson correlation to other N5 libraries, high entropy, low Gini, and low top10 concentration.

In the current historical analysis, `N5_20260401` is a good standard candidate because it has the highest average Pearson correlation to other N5 libraries and the most even abundance distribution.
